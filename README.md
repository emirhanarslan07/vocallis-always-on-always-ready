# Vocallis: Always On, Always Ready

Build a high-converting, premium B2B SaaS landing page called "Vocallis".

Value Proposition:
Vocallis is an 24/7 AI-powered Emergency Dispatcher specifically designed for Water Damage & Disaster Restoration Companies in the US.

The Real Problem We Solve:
Water damage jobs are worth $3,000 to $10,000+ per call. When pipes burst or basements flood at 2:00 AM, homeowners call 3 different companies and hire the first one that answers. If a restoration contractor misses a night call or sends it to voicemail, they lose thousands of dollars instantly.

How Vocallis Works:
1. Zero Missed Emergency Calls: Answers inbound calls on the very first ring, 24/7/365.
2. IICRC-Certified Emergency Triage: The AI asks the critical questions immediately — Is the water still flowing? Clean water or sewage? Is power shut off? Property address & callback number.
3. Instant On-Call Dispatch: Instantly alerts the on-call technician via SMS/Call with full triage details, or escalates if no response.
4. Recovered Revenue Dashboard: Shows owners exactly how many night emergencies were captured and estimated revenue saved (e.g., "$18,500 recovered this month").

Sections Needed:
- Hero: Bold value prop for restoration contractors ("Never Lose a $5,000 Midnight Flood Call Again"), live interactive audio demo / call simulator widget, and a clear CTA ("Start 14-Day Emergency Pilot").
- Live Comparison: Old Way (Voicemail / Sleepy Staff / Expensive answering service) vs. Vocallis Way (0-second answer, instant technician dispatch).
- How the Triage Works: 3-step visual workflow (Homeowner Calls -> AI Triages Severity -> On-Call Tech Woken Up).
- Recovered Jobs Dashboard Preview: High-trust metrics (Emergency Calls Captured, Average Dispatch Time: 45s, Estimated Revenue Saved).
- Simple Pricing: Clear, transparent plans focused on emergency dispatch ($149 Pilot, $299 Starter, $499 24/7 Pro Dispatch).
- High-Converting CTA / Lead Capture: "Try calling our live emergency demo number right now".
